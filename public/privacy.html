Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Reel Rivals LLC ("we") makes three apps, and this one policy covers all of them:

  • Reel Rivals: Movie Trivia for iPhone and iPad
  • Reel Rivals: Movie Night for Apple TV, with the join page at reelrivals.app that players open on their phones
  • Reel Rivals: Headliner for iPhone

We don't sell your information, and none of our apps ask you to create an account with an email address or password.

Reel Rivals: Movie Trivia

Information kept on your device

Your career points, rank, trophies, Lobby Card (favorite movie, genre and tagline), settings, Daily Marquee results and game history are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your player profile

To play friends, you choose a username and can add a first and last name (names are optional). Your profile is saved in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database for this app so other players can find, add and challenge you. It contains your username, any name you entered, your rank and career points, your profile picture choice, the friends you've added, and a player ID. The player ID is an anonymous identifier that iCloud creates for this app; it is not your Apple Account, email address or phone number. Anyone using the app can search for your username or name.

Profile picture, camera and photos

Your profile picture can be your initial, a trophy you've earned, or a photo. If you choose a photo, the app asks for access to your camera or photo library, and only the one photo you pick is uploaded to your profile so your friends can see it. You can switch back to your initial or a trophy at any time.

Friend matches

Head-to-Head and Group Showdown matches are stored in the same iCloud database. A match holds the players' IDs and names, the categories and questions, each player's scores, showcase vehicle and Lobby Card, and short notes such as nudges. The players in a match can see this information. Blocking a player is saved on your device; reports you send come to us by email.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app reminds you about the daily puzzle and matches waiting on you, and alerts you when a friend moves, nudges or challenges you. Friend alerts are delivered through Apple's iCloud and push notification services.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location based on that address, and how you interact with ads. The app asks your permission before allowing tracking for personalized ads; if you decline, ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying Remove Ads turns ads off.

Reel Rivals: Movie Night (Apple TV)

The Apple TV opens a game room on our room service. Players join from their phone's web browser at reelrivals.app with the room code. No app or account is needed. Each player types a display name and picks a vehicle icon. Those names and icons, along with category votes and answers, pass through the room service so everyone's TV and phones stay in step. We use them only to run that game, and a room closes after it has been idle for a while. Like any website, our service receives the phone's or TV's IP address when it connects. Movie Night has no ads.

Reel Rivals: Headliner

Headliner uses your iPhone's motion sensors to tell when you tilt the phone up or down. This happens only on your phone and is not recorded or sent anywhere. Your scores and settings stay on your device.

All apps

Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads, the All-Access Pass, the Movie Night unlock and Headliner packs) are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Leave your name blank and use only a username.
  • Use your initial or a trophy instead of a photo.
  • Turn tracking on or off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Turn notifications on or off in Settings → Notifications → Reel Rivals.
  • Turn off camera or photo access in Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Delete your player profile any time in the app: Profile tab → Delete my profile. This removes your username, name, photo, friends list and online games from iCloud. You can also email us to do it for you.
  • Delete an app to remove the data it stored on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Privacy questions can go to support@reelrivals.app (also on our Support page).
